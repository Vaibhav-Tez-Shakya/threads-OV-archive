Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer and one of the world's most dominant batsmen. Here are the key facts:

**Career Overview:**
- Former captain of the Indian national cricket team (2014–2022 for Test cricket, longer in other formats)
- Widely regarded as one of the greatest modern-day batsmen, often compared to legends like Sachin Tendulkar

**Playing Style:**
- Known for aggressive batting, consistency, and exceptional performances across all formats (Test, ODI, T20)
- Holds numerous records including most international centuries, fastest to reach various milestones

**Achievements:**
- Led India to the 2011 Cricket World Cup victory
- Multiple ICC awards and accolades
- Consistent performer in IPL (Indian Premier League) for Royal Challengers Bangalore

**Off-Field:**
- One of the most recognized athletes globally
- Influential figure in Indian sports and popular culture
- Endorses major international brands

He's been a central figure in Indian cricket for over a decade and remains a key player for both India and his IPL franchise.
