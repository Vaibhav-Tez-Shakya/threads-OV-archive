## Claude



**Mahendra Singh Dhoni** is one of India's greatest cricket captains and most successful batsmen. Key facts:

**Cricket Career:**
- Former captain of the Indian national cricket team (2007–2016)
- Led India to victory in the 2011 Cricket World Cup (ODI format)
- Won the 2007 ICC World Twenty20 as captain
- Known for his aggressive batting style, particularly his finishing ability in limited-overs cricket
- Plays as a wicket-keeper-batsman

**International Record:**
- Over 16,000 runs in ODIs and 4,000+ in Test cricket
- One of the most successful captains in Indian cricket history
- Retired from international cricket in August 2020

**IPL & Club Cricket:**
- Captain of Chennai Super Kings in the Indian Premier League (IPL), a role he continues
- CSK has won multiple IPL titles under his leadership

**Legacy:**
- Considered a brilliant match-finisher with exceptional tactical acumen
- Known by the nickname "Captain Cool" for his calm demeanor under pressure
- Respected for transforming Indian cricket from an underdog mentality to a winning culture

He's widely regarded as one of the greatest limited-overs cricketers of all time and remains an iconic figure in Indian sports.

**Mahendra Singh Dhoni's Major Trophies:**

**International Trophies (As Captain):**

1. **2007 ICC World Twenty20** (T20I) - India's first ICC trophy
2. **2011 ICC Cricket World Cup** (ODI) - India's second World Cup title
3. **2013 ICC Champions Trophy** (ODI)

**Domestic Trophies (IPL - As Captain of Chennai Super Kings):**

1. **2010 IPL** - CSK's first title
2. **2011 IPL** - CSK's second title
3. **2018 IPL** - CSK's third title
4. **2021 IPL** - CSK's fourth title
5. **2023 IPL** - CSK's fifth title

**Other Notable Team Tournament Wins:**
- Multiple bilateral series victories
- Several domestic tournament titles with Jharkhand and Middlesex

**Total Count: Approximately 9 major trophies**

The **3 ICC international trophies** as India's captain are his most prestigious achievements. The **5 IPL titles** with Chennai Super Kings make him one of the most successful IPL captains in league history.
