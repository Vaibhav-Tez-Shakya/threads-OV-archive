## Claude

Baba Ramdev is an Indian yoga teacher, Ayurveda promoter, and businessman. He was born Ramkishan Yadav in Haryana in 1965, and he became widely known through daily televised yoga sessions.

He is best known as a co-founder, with Acharya Balkrishna, of Patanjali Ayurved, a consumer goods and Ayurvedic products company headquartered in Haridwar, Uttarakhand. Through the Divya Yog Mandir trust and its ashram complex, he built a large following and a commercial empire spanning food, personal care, and medicines.

He is also a political and social figure. In 2011 and 2012 he led large anti-corruption protests in New Delhi, demanding the return of Indian money held in foreign banks. Police detained him during the 2012 protests. He has also been involved in public debates on issues like yoga, Ayurveda versus allopathic medicine, and cow protection. In 2024, the Supreme Court of India challenged Patanjali over misleading advertising claims, and Ramdev publicly apologized.
